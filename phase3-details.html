3D Lab Build & CROI

Theme - In-Practice Capability & Commercial Return

Projects

  • Define 3D lab scope (aligners, guides, retainers, in-house appliances)
  • Confirm lab equipment shortlist and supplier quotations
  • Prepare implementation timeline, space plan and compliance checklist
  • Allocate clinical and non-clinical training responsibilities
  • Set baseline outsourcing costs for like-for-like comparison
  • Estimate annual throughput and case mix for the first 12 months
  • Model annual savings and revenue uplift from in-house production
  • Calculate payback period and 3-year CROI scenario
  • Approve go-live milestones and post-launch review cadence

Strategic Review

  • Are projected throughput assumptions still realistic?
  • What is the updated payback period based on live data?
  • Is CROI on track against the approved business case?
  • Which risks are affecting delivery or margin?
  • What optimisation steps improve utilisation and return?

  • A structured approach to continuous improvement.
  • A practical roadmap for sustained growth over the following 12 months.
